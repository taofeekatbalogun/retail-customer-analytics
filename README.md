# Retail Customer Analytics & Trial Store Evaluation

##  Project Overview
This project analyzes retail transaction data to uncover customer purchasing behaviour and evaluate the effectiveness of trial stores.

The goal was to identify key customer segments, understand purchasing patterns, and determine whether trial stores outperformed control stores.

---

##  Key Objectives
- Analyze customer segments and their purchasing behaviour
- Identify key drivers of sales
- Evaluate trial store performance using control store comparison
- Provide data-driven business recommendations

---

##  Tools & Technologies
- Python (Pandas, NumPy)
- Data Visualization (Matplotlib, Seaborn)
- Excel (for additional visualization)

---

##  Key Insights
- Older Families (Budget) and Young Singles/Couples drive the highest sales
- Families tend to purchase in bulk, while younger customers purchase more frequently
- Trial stores showed an increase in sales during the trial period
- The sales uplift was primarily driven by an increase in customer numbers rather than purchase frequency

---

##  Recommendations
- Scale the trial strategy to similar store segments
- Target high-value customer groups with tailored promotions
- Align product offerings with customer purchasing behaviour
